Corporate Overview

Algernon Technology (Tobias Lofton DBA Algernon Technology) is a specialized technology solutions provider and commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) software reseller. We deliver high-throughput data systems, custom software development, and enterprise SaaS license fulfillment for U.S. federal civilian agencies, the Department of Defense, and municipal institutions.

Core Competencies
1. Commercial COTS Software & SaaS Reselling:
  • DevSecOps & Infrastructure: Authorized channel fulfillment for JFrog Artifactory, GitLab, Atlassian (Jira/Confluence), and Red Hat.
  • Clinical & Healthcare SaaS: Specialized clinical pharmacokinetic and therapeutic drug monitoring systems (PrecisePK, DoseMeRx) supporting VA medical centers.
  • Cybersecurity & Observability: Enterprise monitoring and identity tooling including Dynatrace, Splunk, Omnissa Workspace ONE, and Okta.
2. Data Infrastructure & Geospatial Engineering:
  • Large-scale spatial data processing, automated address geocoding, and GIS rooftop centroid extraction cross-referenced with Census TIGERweb APIs.
  • Automated data pipelines, spatial fusion algorithms, and unrestricted Title 13 commingling rights deliverables.
3. Custom Software & Applied AI Systems:
  • Full-stack web application development, deterministic tool-calling AI agent systems, and automated Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers.
Key Institutional Differentiators
  • Rapid RFQ Turnaround: Delivery of compliant firm-fixed-price proposals within 24 to 48 hours of solicitation issuance.
  • 100% NDAA Section 889 Compliant: Zero covered Chinese telecommunications hardware (Huawei, ZTE, Dahua, Hikvision) utilized.
  • Flexible Procurement Vehicles: Direct FAR Part 13 Simplified Acquisition eligibility ($250k SAT), micro-purchase rapid credit card fulfillment (<$10k GPC), and subcontract teaming.
  • Tier-1 Federal Distributor Backing: Directly partnered through national distributor ecosystems (Carahsoft Technology) providing guaranteed OEM warranties and manufacturer support.
