Primary NAICS Classifications

Algernon Technology is registered and operationally equipped to perform work across the following North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) codes:

NAICS 541511

Custom Computer Programming Services

Full-stack custom software development, automated ETL workflows, API bridges, AI agent integration, and custom command dashboards.

NAICS 518210

Computing Infrastructure, Data Processing & Hosting

Automated data collection, high-throughput pipeline hosting, spatial/GIS geocoding verification (TIGERweb / Census API), and automated QA.

NAICS 541512

Computer Systems Design Services

Cloud systems architecture, systems integration, container orchestration, database modernization, and legacy system API bridging.

NAICS 513210

Software Publishers & COTS Software Fulfillment

Fulfillment, licensing, deployment, and technical maintenance for Commercial-Off-The-Shelf (COTS) software products and cloud subscriptions.

Other Computer Related Services

Cybersecurity posture evaluation, automated Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM), DNS identity stack audits, and technical advisory.

Enterprise Software Distribution

COTS Software & SaaS Reseller Capabilities

Algernon Technology acts as an authorized small-business channel reseller and technology fulfillment partner. We connect federal, defense, and civilian agencies with premier commercial software licenses and cloud subscriptions under streamlined SBA set-aside procedures.

DevSecOps & Cloud

Developer & Systems Tooling

Authorized fulfillment for enterprise CI/CD and software supply chain tools including JFrog Artifactory, GitLab, Atlassian (Jira/Confluence), and Red Hat Enterprise Linux. Sourced directly through national Tier-1 federal distributor channels.

Healthcare & Clinical IT

Clinical Decision & Pharmacy SaaS

Specialized distributor relationships for clinical pharmacokinetic dosing platforms (PrecisePK, DoseMeRx) and HIPAA-compliant therapeutic drug monitoring tools for the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) and military health systems.

Cyber & Observability

Security & Cloud Performance

Enterprise monitoring, application performance (APM), and identity management tools including Dynatrace, Splunk, Omnissa Workspace ONE, and Okta with zero-trust compliance.

Rapid Acquisition

Fast RFQ Turnaround & Net 30 Terms

We deliver formal firm-fixed-price SBA proposals within 24 to 48 hours of RFQ release. Full support for WAWF / IPP.gov Net 30 invoicing and immediate Government Purchase Card (GPC Visa/Mastercard) execution for micro-purchases under $10,000.

Procurement Vehicles & Contracting Methods

We support federal contracting officers and prime contractors across standard acquisition frameworks:

FAR Part 13 Simplified Acquisition

Direct award and small-business set-aside acquisition for commercial services and products up to the $250,000 Simplified Acquisition Threshold (SAT).

Micro-Purchase Rapid Fulfillment

Instant government purchase card (GPC) fulfillment for software subscriptions, dataset deliveries, and engineering pilots under the $10,000 micro-purchase limit.

Subcontracting & Prime Teaming

Agile technical subcontractor partnering with Tier-1 defense primes to satisfy small business participation goals on major federal task orders.
