Procurement Vehicles & Contracting Methods

We support federal contracting officers and prime contractors across standard acquisition frameworks:

FAR Part 13 Simplified Acquisition

Direct award and small-business set-aside acquisition for commercial services and products up to the $250,000 Simplified Acquisition Threshold (SAT).

Micro-Purchase Rapid Fulfillment

Instant government purchase card (GPC) fulfillment for software subscriptions, dataset deliveries, and engineering pilots under the $10,000 micro-purchase limit.

Subcontracting & Prime Teaming

Agile technical subcontractor partnering with Tier-1 defense primes to satisfy small business participation goals on major federal task orders.
