Enterprise Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2, 2026 • Algernon Technology

Algernon Technology (“Company”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) operates corporate web platforms, B2B technology evaluation tools, and government contracting fulfillment services. We are committed to maintaining rigorous data privacy, confidentiality, and institutional data governance.

1. Scope and Applicability

This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, and safeguard information across our commercial web interfaces, client engagements, and public sector interactions. We do not sell personal or institutional data to third-party data brokers under any circumstances.

2. Information We Process

We process information only as necessary to fulfill technical inquiries, contract performance, and security services:

  • Commercial Communications: Contact details (name, corporate email, institutional affiliation, telephone number) submitted via capabilities inquiries or contracting correspondence.
  • Public & Technographic Metadata: System metadata, DNS records, and publicly observable security telemetry processed during automated Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) and trust center assessments.
  • Contract Fulfillment Records: Procurement identifiers, purchase order numbers, and licensing records required to process deliveries and satisfy federal reporting under FAR and DFARS standards.

3. Data Protection & Zero-Trust Architecture

We apply defense-in-depth principles across our operational infrastructure:

  • All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3 cryptographic protocols.
  • Sensitive internal databases and deployment credentials are encrypted at rest using AES-256.
  • Our software systems adhere to NIST SP 800-171 cybersecurity standards and strict principle-of-least-privilege access controls.

4. Public Sector & Federal Data Governance

In performing public sector, defense, and institutional contracts, Algernon Technology strictly complies with applicable federal data rights and confidentiality regulations, including Title 13 (U.S. Census Bureau data protection), HIPAA / HITECH healthcare safeguards, and NDAA Section 889 telecommunications restrictions.
